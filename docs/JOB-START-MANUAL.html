Job Start — how it works

Handing a won job from Sales to Operations. Read once, then just use it.

Operations accepts before we mobilize.
No acceptance means no crew, no material, and no promised start date.

The short version

Open portal.greenvalleycontractors.com/ui/jobstart and pick a won bid. Most of the packet is already filled in — it reads your scope review out of Drive, the bid off the Bid Board, and any updates posted on the Projects or Operations boards. You fill the few things it can't know, then send it to Ops. Ops accepts, and the job becomes real everywhere at once.

Nothing is handwritten and nothing gets printed. The packet comes out as a PDF that files itself into the job's Drive folder.

Job names — the standard

One format, everywhere — Monday, Drive, estimates, invoices, CRM, photos:

Street Number Name, City, ST ZIP | Builder | Job Title

RightWrong
9195 Silva Drive, Cincinnati, OH 45241 | Willow Creek | Smith residence 9195 Silva | Willow Creek (missing city/state/ZIP + Job Title)
100 Main Street, Cincinnati, OH 45202 | ABC Builders | First Financial Bank 3776 Susanna – Martin (dash, no city, no Job Title)
CO_9195 Silva Drive, Cincinnati, OH 45241 | Willow Creek | Smith residence 9195 Silva Drive, Cincinnati OH 45241 - Willow Creek - New House

Pipe character, not a dash. Exactly three parts. City, state and ZIP belong in the name — we work in three states and many share the same road names. Job Title is required: residential = {Last} residence; commercial = the business / tenant name. Job type ("New House", "Remodel") still stays in fields on the record. Prefixes we already use stay: CO_, STU-, PL-, WAR-.

Job Start renames it for you. It converts the bid's messy name to the standard and shows you what it changed. If it can't work out the builder or Job Title it will say so and ask — it will never invent one.
Old jobs still match. Everything created before this standard has an old-style name. Job Start recognises those and updates the existing project instead of creating a second one, so you won't get duplicates during the switchover.

Sales — sending a job over

Sales1 · Pick the won bid

Only bids at stage Accepted show up. Anything already waiting on Ops sits at the top of the list, so you can see what's outstanding.

Sales2 · Check what it filled in

A green bar at the top names the scope review it read and what it found — trades in scope, how many exclusions, how many open questions. Each field that was filled automatically has a small tag saying where it came from (from the scope review, from a board update, from the bid).

Read it over and correct anything the document got wrong. Anything you type is never overwritten by the automatic sources.

No scope review found? It says so and falls back to the bid. Write the scope review into the job folder and reload — it'll pick it up.

Sales3 · Fill the eight required fields

The bar at the bottom counts what's left ("3 still needed"). The eight are:

Project typeResidential or Commercial
BuilderWho we're working for on site
Site supervisorName and number — who the crew calls
Scope of workWhat we sold
What we did NOT sellExclusions. The field that saves us.
Start dateBest known — Ops reschedules from there
Board countOps stocks from this number
Lock box / accessDay-one blocker. Say "none needed" if the site is open.
It saves as you type. Start it on your phone in a driveway, lose signal, finish at your desk. You'll never retype a packet.

Sales4 · Email the scope to the GC (optional)

Skip anytime. This does not block Send to Operations. The eight required fields above are enough to hand off.

When you use it: tap Draft the scope confirmation. It writes an email to the GC's PM (copying the site super) with our scope, our exclusions, the start date, and a 3-business-day reply window. It also tells them in writing that our field crew doesn't price work — so the super stops asking our foreman for numbers.

It lands in hello@ Drafts. Read it, then send it yourself. Nothing sends automatically.

Worth doing when you can — every correction a GC sends back is a change order we didn't eat. Not required to start the handoff.

The email strips square footage and "1-side / 2-side" phrasing automatically, per our bid-description rules — the scope review is an internal document and is full of both.

Sales5 · Send to Operations

The button unlocks once the eight are filled — scope confirmation is not part of that count. It builds the packet PDF and pings Ops in Slack. Nothing is written to Monday yet — the job isn't real until Ops accepts it.

Operations — accepting a job

OpsRead it, then accept or send it back

You'll get a Slack message: 📋 Job packet ready for Operations, with a link to the packet PDF. Open the job in Job Start and you'll see the whole packet plus an Open questions section — the items the scope review flagged as unresolved. Those are the ones that turn into change orders if nobody chases them.

  • Accept this job — everything below happens at once.
  • Send back — type what's missing. It goes back to Sales with your note, editable again, and the reason is posted in Slack. No form to fill out.
You can't accept a packet you sent. Two people, always — that's the whole point of the acceptance step.

What happens the moment you accept

  • The Projects item is created (or updated, if one already exists)
  • The Operations task is created — this is the one that never used to happen
  • The bid is stamped with the Accepted Date and linked to both boards
  • The accepted packet PDF is filed to the job's Handoff/ folder in Drive
  • Slack gets one message with links to all four

Common questions

The packet is mostly blank.

No scope review was found for that job. Check it's in the job folder and that the name shares the street number with the bid — that's what it matches on.

It says Monday rejected the Job Location or Customer.

Known Monday API limitation, not a portal fault. The job was still created — set those two fields by hand on the project.

I need to change an accepted packet.

Accepted packets are a record, not a form. Edit the Projects or Operations item directly, or use Job Check for field updates.

A field is wrong and it came from the scope review.

Just type over it. Typed values always win, and they stick.

Can I hand off the same job twice?

Yes, safely. It updates the existing project and ops task in place rather than creating duplicates.

Still true today: the packet records the date the GC confirmation was drafted, not when it was sent — nobody can tell the portal when you hit send. Send it the same day you draft it and the date stays honest.

Who to tell when it breaks

Anything that looks wrong goes to Jordan with the job name and what you expected. Portal errors post themselves to #portal-bugs-and-improvements automatically, so don't worry about capturing screenshots.
