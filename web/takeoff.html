How the money path clicks through
  1. Takeoff — measure boards / scope; Export or Send to Portal Queue.
  2. Estimate Generator — import JSON or resume the staged draft; preview; Accept → hello@ draft.
  3. Job Start — when the bid is won, hand the packet to Ops (Ops accepts).
